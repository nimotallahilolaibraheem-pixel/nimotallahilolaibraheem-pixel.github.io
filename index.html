A little about me

I’m a Nigeria-based writer and content creator. I began publishing my writing online in August 2022 and have continued to explore how words can inform, encourage, and help people see familiar subjects from a new perspective.

My interests include reflective essays, faith-centred writing, health communication, and lifestyle content. As a nursing student, I’m developing my understanding of health topics and enjoy translating what I learn into accessible, carefully researched writing.

I also run Nimah Exquisite Collection, an online business that has given me practical experience with product presentation, customer communication, and writing for a small brand.

I approach health topics as a student writer and communicator, not as a substitute for professional medical advice.
